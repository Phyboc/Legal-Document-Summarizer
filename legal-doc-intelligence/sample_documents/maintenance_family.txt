The Judgment of the Court was delivered by FERNANDES, J.

The appellant wife filed a petition under Section 125 of the Code of Criminal
Procedure seeking maintenance for herself and her minor daughter from the
respondent husband. The dispute concerns the quantum of maintenance and the
wife's entitlement to it.

The parties were married in 2009 and separated in 2018. The Family Court
awarded Rs. 6,000 per month to the wife and Rs. 4,000 to the daughter. The
wife appeals for enhancement, while the husband disputes his liability
altogether.

Learned counsel for the appellant wife contended that the husband, who runs a
wholesale grocery business, has an income far exceeding what he disclosed, and
that the amount awarded is inadequate to maintain the standard of living
enjoyed during the marriage. She submitted that the daughter's school fees
alone exceed the sum granted.

The respondent husband submitted that the wife is a qualified teacher capable
of earning her own livelihood and that she left the matrimonial home without
sufficient cause, disentitling her to maintenance.

We have considered the submissions. In our opinion, the mere capacity of a
wife to earn is not a ground to deny maintenance where she is not in fact
employed, and the evidence shows the wife left only after persistent
ill-treatment. The Court finds that the husband's lifestyle and business
turnover indicate an income well above his admitted figure.

The maintenance is enhanced to Rs. 15,000 per month for the wife and
Rs. 10,000 for the daughter, payable from the date of the petition. The appeal
is allowed.
