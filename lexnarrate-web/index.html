What LexNarrate does / 05

Built for clarity you can trust.

Every feature is source-grounded: summaries map back to the document, and unverifiable claims are flagged rather than invented.

Dual summaries

Lawyer Mode keeps the section-aware legal structure; Citizen Mode rewrites it in plain language.

Cite & Verify

An NLI model checks each claim against the source and marks it supported, unsupported, or contradicted.

Outcome preservation

The operative order is routed to its own judgement section so the holding — ALLOWED / DISMISSED — is never dropped.

Section routing

A rule + classifier splits facts, arguments, reasoning and judgement before summarizing each with its own budget.

PII redaction

Aadhaar, PAN, phone and email patterns are detected and can be redacted before anything is summarized.

Ask & search

Grounded Q&A answers only from the uploaded document, with full-text search and JSON / Markdown / text export.

How it works / 06

From a dense judgment to a clear explanation.

Six stages run on every document — the same pipeline the research was built and evaluated on.

01

Upload & extract

Drop a PDF, DOCX or TXT. Text is extracted and cleaned of court-PDF artefacts.

02

Clean & redact

Preamble is stripped and PII patterns are optionally redacted.

03

Detect sections

Paragraphs are labelled facts / arguments / reasoning / judgement, keeping the operative order intact.

04

Summarize

A fine-tuned BART model summarizes each chunk with a section-specific token budget.

05

Aggregate & format

Chunk summaries are merged and rendered as Lawyer Mode and plain-language Citizen Mode.

06

Cite & Verify

Each sentence is checked against the source; grounded extraction fills dates, parties, provisions and outcome.

Explainability · XAI / 09

The model has to show its work.

Every claim is grounded by an explainable-AI check, and that check is itself evaluated against deliberately corrupted summaries — so we can measure how reliably it catches a hallucination.

The algorithm — NLI entailment attribution

Cite & Verify treats each generated sentence as a hypothesis and the source passage it was drawn from as the premise, then runs a natural-language-inference model (nli-deberta-v3). The entailment probability becomes a per-sentence grounding score: high means the document supports the claim, low means it is unsupported or contradicted. It is attribution you can read sentence by sentence — not a single opaque confidence number.

How it is evaluated — an adversarial faithfulness probe

We built 634 claim–source pairs across 40 test judgments: 239 genuinely supported sentences plus four families of planted hallucinations — negation, number-swap, outcome-flip and cross-document claims. A faithful explainer should score the real ones high and the corrupted ones low. Operating point: entailment threshold τ = 0.45.

Frequently asked / 10

Questions, answered.

The scope, safeguards and limits of LexNarrate — the things worth knowing before you trust a summary.

No. LexNarrate is an AI tool that explains documents — it is not a lawyer and does not give legal advice, opinions or representation. Every summary carries a reminder to have it reviewed by a qualified legal professional before you rely on it.
It is built and evaluated on Indian court judgments (the IN-ABS dataset) and accepts PDF, DOCX or TXT. Text is extracted, court-PDF artefacts are cleaned, and the case-caption preamble can be stripped before anything is summarized.
Two ways. Section routing pins the operative order to its own judgement chunk so the holding is never dropped, and Cite & Verify runs an NLI model over every sentence to score it against the source — unsupported or contradicted claims are flagged rather than shown as fact. The check itself is measured (ROC-AUC 0.891; see Explainability).
Lawyer Mode preserves the section-aware legal structure — facts, arguments, reasoning and judgement with their legal phrasing. Citizen Mode rewrites the same content in plain, everyday language. Both are produced from the same grounded summary and both are run through Cite & Verify.
No. Documents are processed in the session to produce the summary and are not retained or used to train public models. Aadhaar, PAN, phone and email patterns can be detected and redacted before summarization, and no PII patterns survived on the evaluation build.
